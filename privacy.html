Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Who we are

Pecan is a student project started at Imperial College London. It is not a company and does not offer a service to the public. This website exists to show what we are building.

What this website collects

Nothing. The site has no forms, no sign-up, no accounts and no contact details to submit, so we do not collect, store or receive any personal data through it.

Cookies

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers, and does not store anything on your device.

Hosting

The site is hosted by Netlify. Like any web host, Netlify processes your IP address and browser details to deliver pages and protect against abuse, under its own privacy policy. Everything else, including fonts, is served from this site. We do not have access to visitor-level data.

Changes

If Pecan starts collecting any personal data, for example through a contact form, accounts or analytics, we will update this page first, and we will ask for your consent before using any non-essential cookies.
